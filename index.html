Why choose CGA

JAMB / UTME preparation that starts with the exam itself

Good results come from understanding the exam and practising for it. We help students know what to expect and prepare step by step. We do not promise scores or admission; we help you prepare properly.

Enrol for JAMB/UTME
  • Understand the examination environment
  • Practise with CBT-style questions
  • Improve speed and accuracy
  • Build confidence
  • Learn structured exam strategies
  • Track your preparation progress
  • Get guided academic support

CBT practice that feels like the real thing

Many students lose marks to unfamiliarity, not knowledge. Regular practice on a computer helps you get comfortable with:

  • Computer-based testing
  • Moving between questions
  • Managing your time
  • Answering under timed conditions
  • Basic computer interaction
  • Examination confidence
